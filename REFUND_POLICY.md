# LinuxFIX Payment and Refund Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

## Current model

LinuxFIX is currently a free service. The application does not sell subscriptions, digital features, or products, so it does not collect payments or issue refunds.

## Future payments

If paid features are introduced, users will receive information about the price, billing period, automatic renewal, cancellation method, and refund rules before making a purchase. This document will be updated before sales begin.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play will also be subject to the applicable store's refund rules and mandatory consumer law. This policy does not limit rights that cannot legally be excluded by contract.

For payment-related questions, contact LinuxFIXcontact@proton.me.
